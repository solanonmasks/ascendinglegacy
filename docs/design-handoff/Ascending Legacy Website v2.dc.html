Super Visa insurance, done right the first time.

A Super Visa application must include proof of private medical insurance from a Canadian insurer: at least $100,000 in emergency coverage, valid for at least one year from the date of entry. We arrange it, issue the proof-of-coverage letter, and explain what the policy does and does not cover before you pay.
