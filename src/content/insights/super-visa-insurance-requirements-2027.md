The Super Visa lets parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents visit for long stays. Medical insurance is one of the requirements, and it is the one that most often causes delays. Here is what the application needs, in plain language.

## The four things the policy must do

1. **Come from an eligible insurer.** The policy must be from a Canadian insurance company, or in some cases an insurer outside Canada that IRCC accepts. We arrange coverage through Canadian carriers.
2. **Cover at least $100,000.** That is the minimum for emergency medical care. Many families choose more.
3. **Last at least one year from the date of entry.** The policy must be valid for a full year from the day your parent or grandparent arrives in Canada.
4. **Cover health care, hospital care and repatriation.** In other words, emergency treatment while they are here and getting them home if needed.

You will also need proof that the policy has been bought, or quoted with payment arranged. That is the proof-of-coverage letter we send for the application.

## Why applications get delayed

- **The letter does not match the application.** Names, dates of birth and the entry date need to match exactly.
- **The coverage starts on the wrong date.** It should start on the day of arrival, not the day you buy it.
- **The policy is for less than a year.** A six-month policy will not meet the requirement, even with a plan to renew.

## What it costs

The price depends on the visitor's age, the coverage amount, the deductible, and whether pre-existing conditions are covered. A higher deductible lowers the premium. Most carriers offer monthly payments.

## If the visa is refused

Most policies refund in full with proof of refusal. If your parent leaves Canada early, unused days are usually refunded as long as no claim was made.

## Check the current rules

IRCC can update the Super Visa requirements at any time. Always check the official Super Visa page on canada.ca before you apply, and call us if anything is unclear. We will tell you the number on the first call.
